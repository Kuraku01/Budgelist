        Developed by Kuraku01

        v0.2.1-beta

        • Multi-Layer Android Back Navigation: Back gestures and buttons now close active UI layers one by one (dropdowns, date popovers, modals, sheets) with double-back confirmation to exit on the root screen.
        • Integrated Update Center: Separate Stable Update and Testing Build channels, non-blocking startup update notifications, and a unified Update Center in Settings → About.
        • Faster & More Reliable Update Checks: Instant update checks without hourly rate limits, plus a 10-minute cooldown on background startup checks.
        • Automatic App Upgrade Cleanup: Automatically refreshes cached updates whenever a newer Android app installation is detected.

        v0.2.0-beta

        • Two-Tier Global & Monthly Budget System: Manage an overall Budget pool with a detailed addition history log, and lend monthly budgets using a synchronized input, smooth slider, and preset chips.
        • Automatic Past-Month Settlement & Lock: Unspent monthly budget automatically returns once to your Budget when a month ends, while past months lock to preserve historical records.
        • Interactive Calendar & Custom Date Picker: Full month/year dropdowns with auto-expanding year ranges (1970–+50 yrs), day-level filtering, and a custom dropdown calendar in the Add Expense sheet.
        • Color Themes & UI Polish: Choose from 6 color themes (Violet, Emerald, Ocean, Rose, Sunset, Slate) in Light or Dark mode, plus tactile 3D FAB depth and app-wide text selection prevention.
        • Universal Live OTA Engine & Security Hardening: Full HTML/CSS/JS over-the-air updates, upgraded Capacitor 8.6.0 & AGP 9.5.0, and hardened CSV export validation.

        v0.1.2-beta

        • Upgraded Forecast Algorithm: Multi-factor forecasting incorporating upcoming unpaid recurring bills and multi-month spend velocity baselines.
        • Safe Daily Spend Target: Real-time daily variable spend allowance to keep your budget on track.
        • Multi-Tier Budget Warnings: Intelligent alerts for critical over-budget spending, near-budget caution, and on-track status.
        • Budget Reference Line: Visual dashed reference line on the forecast chart corresponding to your monthly budget limit.
        • Seamless In-App Updates: Get the latest features and bug fixes automatically when opening the app.
        • Update Progress Indicator: See real-time download and installation progress directly on the startup screen.
        • Password Toggle: Tap the eye icon on password fields to view or hide what you type.
        • Performance & Security Improvements: Enhanced stability, smoother list rendering, and upgraded data protection.

        v0.1.0-beta

        • Local-First Offline Mode: Instant 0ms app open & local expense logging.
        • Cloud Sync & Pull-to-Refresh: Background and pull-down gestures to sync across devices.
        • Guest Mode: Instant offline guest access without registration.
        • Username & Email Auth: Sign up with a unique username and log in via email or username.
        • Realtime Network Status: Floating network status pill indicating connection state.
        • Compact Settings: Collapsible categories, avatar presets, and about section.
        • CSV Export: Direct CSV export to your Downloads folder.
